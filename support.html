Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my "Remove Ads" purchase?

Go to Settings (gear icon on the home screen) and tap "Restore Purchases". Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

The app isn't playing videos. What should I do?

Make sure your device isn't in silent mode and that the volume is turned up. Also check that "Sound Effects" and "Background Music" are enabled in the app's Settings.

What age group is this app for?

Spell Quest Kids is designed for children ages 3-6 (preschool and kindergarten). It features 3 difficulty levels to grow with your child's abilities.

How many words are in the app?

The app currently includes 45+ words across 3 difficulty levels (Easy, Medium, Hard), each with animated question and answer video clips.

Is an internet connection required?

An internet connection is only needed for displaying ads. The spelling game itself works fully offline.

How do I remove ads?

You can remove all ads with a one-time purchase of $2.99 from the Settings screen or when prompted in the app.

Which devices are supported?

Spell Quest Kids works on all iPhones and iPads running iOS 17.0 or later.
