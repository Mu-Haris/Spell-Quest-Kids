Frequently Asked Questions

What does Premium include?

Spell Quest Premium is a one-time purchase that removes all ads and unlocks the Medium, Hard, and Random word packs. The price is shown in the app in your local currency before you buy. There are no subscriptions or recurring charges.

How do I restore my Premium purchase?

Go to Settings (gear icon on the home screen) and tap "Restore Purchases", or tap "Restore Purchases" on the Premium card. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase. Restoring is always free. Earlier "Remove Ads" purchases are restored as Premium.

Who is Spell Quest for?

Spell Quest is a general-audience spelling and vocabulary game for early readers, English-language learners, and families. The Easy, Medium, and Hard packs let players move on to longer, trickier words at their own pace.

How many words are in the app?

The app includes 84 words across the Easy, Medium, and Hard packs, plus a Random mix of all of them. Every word has its own animated question and answer video.

The app isn't playing sound. What should I do?

Make sure your device isn't in silent mode and that the volume is turned up. Also check that "Sound Effects" and "Background Music" are turned on in the app's Settings.

Is an internet connection required?

No. The spelling game works fully offline. An internet connection is only used to show ads and to make or restore purchases.

How do I change my ad and tracking choices?

You can change whether apps may track you at any time in your device's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, you can also change your ad consent inside the app under Settings > Privacy Policy > Ad Privacy Choices. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Which devices are supported?

Spell Quest works on iPhone and iPad running iOS 17.0 or later.
