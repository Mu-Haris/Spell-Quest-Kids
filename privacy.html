Privacy Policy

Spell Quest Kids

Last updated: April 9, 2025

Introduction

Kinex Apps ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Spell Quest Kids mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our App.

Information We Collect

Spell Quest Kids does not directly collect any personal information from users. However, our third-party partners may collect certain data as described below.

Third-Party Services

Our App uses the following third-party services:

Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads depending on your consent preferences. This may include:

  • Device identifiers (IDFA, if authorized via App Tracking Transparency)
  • IP address
  • General location (country/region level)
  • App usage data for ad performance

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Apple StoreKit

We use Apple's StoreKit framework for in-app purchases. Purchase transactions are handled entirely by Apple. We do not collect or store any payment information. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

App Tracking Transparency

On iOS 14.5 and later, we request your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before collecting your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). You can change this permission at any time in your device's Settings > Privacy > Tracking.

Data Storage

The App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Sound and music preferences (on/off)
  • In-app purchase status (ads removed)
  • Selected difficulty level

This data is stored using standard iOS mechanisms (UserDefaults) and is not transmitted to any server.

Children's Privacy

While Spell Quest Kids is designed for children ages 3-6, the App is not categorized as a "Kids" app on the App Store. We display standard advertisements and use the App Tracking Transparency framework for consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

In-App Purchases

The App offers a one-time in-app purchase to remove all advertisements ($2.99 USD). This purchase is non-consumable and can be restored on any device signed into the same Apple ID.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
